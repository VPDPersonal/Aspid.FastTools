# Sample recordings, dark and light

Real Unity 6000.4 camera recordings with the shared Dark and Light previews: the Light one uses a neutral gray platform/background, mint and pale blue accents, subdued grid and lifted animated colors. Authored gameplay runs normally: Types shows regular/elite waves moving to the center plus the placed Archer (3 seconds), SerializeReferences shows real attacks and changing dummy health (6 seconds), ProfilerMarkers shows the flock simulation (6 seconds, deterministic seed 42).

Camera sources are lossless FFV1, 1440 × 810 at 20 fps, one per theme (`<slug>-dark.mkv`, `<slug>-light.mkv`). `record.sh <project> <Sample> <Dark|Light>` records one in a hidden batch-mode Editor (asp-unity-capture skill, `editor.sh open`): `prepare.cs.txt` opens the scene in that theme preview, `record.cs.txt` writes the frames in Play Mode. SerializeReferences and ProfilerMarkers replay identically in every run; the Types Archer has a 12-second lifetime, so record both Types themes back to back. The recording helper hides scene title/footer TextMeshes only during each camera render to keep them outside the cropped gameplay illustration, then restores them immediately. The full scene PNGs here keep the titles; the site gallery's scene previews and clips are built without them by `docs/media/samples-gallery/scenes.py`. Crops, the same for both themes: Types 1204×680 at (100,116), SerializeReferences 888×532 at (276,178), ProfilerMarkers 800×688 at (320,108). No image recoloring or compositing.

The Ability Catalog states in `catalog/<theme>/` are grabs of the real AbilityCatalogWindow at 2x, 1000 × 563 pt, from a hidden batch-mode Editor, in the window's own Theme → Dark / Light; `catalog.cs.txt` has the steps. The real **Halve cooldown, +5 MP** handler changes Arcane Ward from 15 MP / 12 s to 20 MP / 6 s, and Undo returns 15 / 12 (the undone grab equals `idle.png` byte for byte, so the specs reuse it). `demo.gif` / `demo-light.gif` (2000 × 888, the body under the header) are composed from those states with the skill's `compose.py catalog/<theme>/spec.json <frames>` and `encode.sh <frames> <gif>`: one timeline at 25 fps, with a composited cursor. The gallery previews are the idle grab with the header, scaled to 1440 × 810.

The ProfilerMarkers page's `profiler-markers.png` / `profiler-markers-light.png` (1102 × 242, the column header and the six Flock rows) are 2x grabs of the real Profiler window, CPU module in Hierarchy view, from a hidden batch-mode Editor in the dark and light editor skins; `profiler-hierarchy.cs.txt` has the step, the column widths and the crop. Both skins show the same recorded frame, so even the timings match.

Run `python3 docs/media/sample-themes/other-samples/encode.py` from any directory to reproduce the scene GIFs and the Ability Catalog gallery previews. GIFs use 256 colors and sierra2_4a dithering. Existing media GUIDs are preserved.
